Privacy & Terms

Last updated October 16, 2026

The short version

Daily Climb is a free word game made by Tate Cutcliffe. It collects only what it needs to run the game, show leaderboards and get better. It doesn't sell your information, and there are no ads in the game.

What Daily Climb collects

  • Your profile: your username and your secret emoji, so you can sign in on other devices.
  • Your games: the words you play, your points, your times, your streak and your trophies.
  • Your device: the kind of device and browser, your time zone, and whether you use the Home Screen app. This keeps your daily on your own local time and helps fix bugs.
  • Reminders, if you turn them on: a notification token for your device and the time you chose.
  • Feedback you send: your message, with your username and the game version.
  • Share pictures: when you share a result, its picture is stored at share.thedailyclimb.com for 30 days so the link works, then deleted.
  • How you found the game: for example a shared link, an invite or an ad, recorded once on your first visit.
  • Usage: Google Analytics for Firebase records things like which screens are opened and when a daily is finished. Google may estimate a rough location from your connection.

The game also keeps your progress and settings on your own device, in your browser's storage.

What other players can see

Your username, your results (points, times, and the words you played, shown only to players who have finished that day's puzzle) and your standings on the daily and weekly leaderboards. Choose a username you're happy for others to see.

Who helps run it

  • Google Firebase: the game's database, sign-in, analytics and notifications.
  • Google reCAPTCHA: checks that requests come from the real game and not a bot. It looks at things like your browser and device. This site is protected by reCAPTCHA and the Google Privacy Policy and Terms of Service apply.
  • Cloudflare: share links and pictures.
  • GitHub Pages: hosting the game itself.

They process information only to provide those services.

Your choices

  • Turn reminders off at any time from the ☰ menu, or in your device's settings.
  • To delete your profile and your results, email support@thedailyclimb.com from any address with your username. It's done within 30 days.
  • Questions about your information go to the same address.

Children

Daily Climb isn't directed at children under 13, and doesn't knowingly collect information from them. If you think a child has made a profile, email support@thedailyclimb.com and it will be removed.

Terms of play

  • Daily Climb is free and provided as it is, without guarantees. It may change, pause or stop.
  • Play fair: no bots, scripts or tampering with scores. Results or profiles that break this may be removed.
  • Usernames must not be offensive, hateful or pretend to be someone else. These may be renamed or removed.
  • The game, its puzzles and its look belong to Daily Climb.

Changes and contact

If this page changes, the date at the top changes too, and important changes are posted in the game's Announcements. Questions: support@thedailyclimb.com.
